## How this list was made

- **Prepared early.** Atul asked for this before milestone LM0 (the pilot review, L0.10). The scores use the pilot's ten criteria (`PMC12949993/PILOT_CASE.md`, "Why this case") and may shift once the pilot review settles how much each criterion matters.
- **Already chosen, so left out:** the pilot PMC12949993 and Sambhasha's four starter cases (PMC11227049, PMC12007988, PMC11227436, PMC11890614). PMC12611801 and PMC13089664, scored in `PILOT_CASE.md`, were re-checked and kept.
- **Search (25 Sep 2026).** NCBI E-utilities only (`esearch`, `esummary` and `efetch`, `db=pmc`, `tool=case-library`), with at most 3 requests per second. Every topic query was combined with `"open access"[filter] AND ("case reports"[pt] OR "case report"[tiab]) AND 2023:2026[pdat]`. There were 25 topic queries:
  - Round 1: drug-induced immune haemolysis (25 hits), G6PD (37), PNH (94), TTP mimics (15), megaloblastic anaemia (75), iron deficiency with a twist (76), sideroblastic anaemia (3), copper and zinc (21), thalassaemia and haemoglobinopathies (98), myeloma and plasma cell disorders "misdiagnosed/mimicking" (88), lymphoma presenting oddly (494), HLH with infection triggers (178), kala-azar, malaria, scrub typhus and dengue with haematological features (94), toxic causes such as lead, arsenic, methaemoglobinaemia and dapsone (100), acquired haemophilia and acquired von Willebrand disease (168), vitamin K deficiency, superwarfarin and snakebite (75), and India-affiliated anaemia with a marrow or film (43).
  - Round 2: iron deficiency with menorrhagia (24), B12 in vegetarians (14), thalassaemia trait against iron deficiency (18), India or Indian in title or abstract (111), hookworm, sprue and coeliac disease (46), cold agglutinins (35), Wilson disease haemolysis (7), and Coombs-negative or hereditary haemolysis in adults (9).
- **Screening.** 1,948 hits in total. The first 50–60 records of each query (PMC returns the newest first) gave **923 unique records, screened by title and journal**. About 60 abstracts were read. **39 full texts** were fetched as JATS XML and cached under `case-library/data/articles/<PMCID>/efetch.jats.xml` (git-ignored); 35 search responses are cached under `data/articles/_searches/`.
- **Licences.** PMC's OA web service (`oa.fcgi`) returned HTTP 404 on the day, so **each licence was read from the article's JATS `<permissions><license>` element on 25 Sep 2026** (the `ali:license_ref` or the Creative Commons link in `<license-p>`). The "Licence" column quotes that URL. All 39 fetched articles carry a Creative Commons licence; none is CC BY-SA. Re-verify each on the PMC page at L1.2, as for the starter set.
- **Flags** follow SPEC §9. CC BY: `production_ok` yes, `public_release_ok` yes. CC BY-NC: no, and yes (non-commercial only). CC BY-NC-ND: no and no, so these are usable for development and internal testing only. CC BY-SA would be "only if Atul accepts"; none turned up.
- **Figure credits.** None of the 39 fetched articles has a figure-level `<attrib>` or `<permissions>`, and no caption says "reproduced", "adapted from", "courtesy" or "with permission". So the article licence covers the figures as published. The one exception to check is noted in the table (a product photograph).

Score key, as in the pilot: 9–10 is pilot quality; 8–8.5 is strong; 7–7.5 is usable with gaps; 6–6.5 is a reserve or needs a lot of filling. "Consultants" means the referrals a team would plausibly make in play.
